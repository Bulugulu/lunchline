# Case Study Overview

## Deadline
**June 1, 2026 EOD** (ask in advance if more time needed)

## Deliverables

### Part 1: Public Company Investment Pitch
- 10-12 page PowerPoint
- Supporting financial/returns model (Excel)
- PDF copies welcome

**Slide Order:**
1. Business Overview (2-3 pages): what they do, revenue model, customers, geography, history
2. End Market Overview (1 page): TAM, growth, structure, fragmentation, secular drivers
3. Competitive Positioning and Dynamics (1 page): who wins, who loses, why
4. Investment Thesis (1-2 pages): 3-5 reasons this works
5. Investment Risks (1 page): the 3 things that kill the trade
6. Value Creation Plan (2-3 pages): commercial, operations, capital structure, M&A
7. Financial Review (1-2 pages): historical financials, unit economics, valuation framework, returns sensitivity

**Company Criteria:**
- Publicly traded on NYSE or Nasdaq
- Current Enterprise Value between $10M and $500M
- Messy, mispriced, or under-followed preferred
- Avoid obvious names
