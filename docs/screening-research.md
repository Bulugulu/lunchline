## Tier 1 Candidates (Software/SaaS/AI, Strong Sector Fit)

### MIND C.T.I. Ltd (NASDAQ: MNDO) — EV ~$25M
- Billing/customer care software for telecoms
- Cash-rich, zero debt, ~13% FCF yield, Acquirer's Multiple 3.67
- Intrinsic value estimated ~2x current price
- **Angle:** "Too small to cover" deep-value software compounder. Potential acquirer target.

### Definitive Healthcare (NASDAQ: DH) — EV ~$100-$150M
- SaaS healthcare commercial intelligence platform
- Revenue ~$220M but market cap dropped ~48% in one year
- Data/analytics on healthcare providers for GTM planning
- **Angle:** Vertical SaaS with massive revenue-to-EV disconnect. Market pricing in significant risk — worth investigating if platform has durable value.

### Marchex (NASDAQ: MCHX) — EV ~$70M
- AI-powered conversational intelligence/analytics for multi-location businesses
- Revenue ~$45M, market cap ~$70M
- Products: AI call analytics (Spotlight), generative AI call summaries
- **Angle:** Pure-play AI/analytics priced like a legacy company. Product background gives edge in evaluating competitive position.

### Domo (NASDAQ: DOMO) — EV ~$250M
- BI/analytics and AI data platform
- Revenue ~$318M, trading at 0.8x EV/Revenue
- Board initiated strategic alternatives process (Feb 2026)
- **Angle:** Active M&A catalyst. Real revenue, question is competitive positioning vs. larger BI players.

### eGain (NASDAQ: EGAN) — EV ~$115M
- AI knowledge hub and customer engagement SaaS
- Market cap ~$192M, EV ~$115M (cash-rich)
- Revenue growing 7% YoY, profitable
- **Angle:** Profitable AI SaaS at modest valuation. Founded 1997, positioned for enterprise AI wave.

### Smith Micro Software (NASDAQ: SMSI) — EV ~$21M
- Software for wireless carriers and cable operators
- Family digital lifestyle solutions
- **Angle:** Extremely small. Need to assess business viability, but tiny EV = high upside if technology has strategic value.

### Intellicheck (NASDAQ: IDN) — EV ~$146M
- Identity verification SaaS for financial services, fintech, e-commerce
- Revenue up 13% YoY, SaaS 91% gross margin, profitable
- Pivoting from retail toward banking, title insurance, auto
- **Angle:** High-margin growing SaaS at small valuation. Identity/fraud is a large market.

### LivePerson (NASDAQ: LPSN) — Market cap ~$27M, EV ~$319M
- AI conversational platform for enterprise digital engagement
- Revenue ~$236M but losing ~$62M annually
- Once a $4B+ company
- **Angle:** Classic messy situation. Real AI tech and revenue, question is whether debt/burn manageable. High risk.

### Veritone (NASDAQ: VERI) — Small market cap
- Enterprise AI software platform (aiWARE) for unstructured data
- Revenue ~$130-$145M guided, stock down 63% YTD
- Recent multi-year Oracle Cloud Infrastructure deal
- **Angle:** AI platform at distressed levels. Oracle deal validates technology. Messy.

### GSE Systems (NASDAQ: GVP) — Market cap ~$16M
- Simulation, training, engineering for power/process industries
- **Angle:** Tiny industrial vertical software. Niche but real customers.

## Key Observations
1. **Vertical SaaS micro-caps are thinning** — PE has taken many private (Instructure, EngageSmart, Cantaloupe, Brightcove, Sharecare). Remaining public ones may be next.
2. **Strategic alternatives processes** (Domo) create event-driven opportunities aligned with M&A background.
3. **Healthcare data/analytics** and **conversational AI** are sectors where product management expertise gives genuine edge.
4. **"Below cash" / negative EV** situations are tracked by The Value Road and The Acquirer's Multiple — worth monitoring.
