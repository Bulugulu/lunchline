Item 5.07         Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.
 
On April 23, 2024, Art’s-Way Manufacturing, Co., Inc. (the “Company”) held its 2024 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). At the Annual Meeting, the following actions were taken:
 
1. The Stockholders elected five nominees to the Company’s Board of Directors to hold office until the next annual meeting and until their successors are elected and qualified.
 
2. The Stockholders ratified the selection of Eide Bailly LLP as the Company's independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending November 30, 2024.
 
3. The Stockholders approved, on a non-binding and advisory basis, the compensation of the named executive officers of the Company.
