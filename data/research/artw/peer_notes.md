# ARTW Peer Selection Rationale

**Date:** 2026-05-30 · For: Art's-Way Manufacturing (ARTW) Deep Flow carve-out thesis.

## The comp problem

ARTW has **no clean public pure-play comp.** It is two unlike businesses stapled together:
1. A cyclical, loss-making **farm-equipment** job shop (Ag Products), and
2. A profitable (~17% op margin), project-based **custom modular-building fabricator** specializing in research labs / biocontainment / animal facilities (Art's-Way Scientific).

The carve-out thesis values (2) standalone. The relevant universe is therefore *specialty/custom modular building fabricators* and *founder-controlled profitable health-adjacent small-caps* — neither of which has a tight US public pure-play. So I triangulate from three anchors and document why each is partial.

## The anchors

### 1. FONR (Fonar) — the PRECEDENT TRANSACTION (the most load-bearing anchor)
Not a trading comp; a **completed take-private** that mirrors the ARTW situation almost exactly: a founder/family-controlled (the Damadian family), profitable, healthcare-adjacent small-cap taken private **Dec 23, 2025** by a **CEO-led** group at **$19.00/share = ~$98.62M**, a **31.5% premium** to prior close (39.7% over the 90-day average), at roughly **0.94x revenue / ~7–9x EBITDA**. This is the single best read on (a) what a friendly, control-holder-led take-out of a neglected profitable micro-cap clears at, and (b) the premium an outside acquirer must pay over the unaffected price. It directly informs the whole-company acquisition-return math.
*Source: [FONR 8-K/DEFA14A 2025-12-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000355019/000173112225001734/fonar_exhibit-99.htm); [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/FONR/); [InsideArbitrage](https://www.insidearbitrage.com/2025/12/fonar-corp-to-be-acquired-by-ceo-led-group-for-98-62-million-in-cash/).*

### 2. WSC (WillScot) — SECONDARY, the "modular" ceiling (with a big asterisk)
The only sizeable public "modular" name, but it is a **rental/leasing fleet** business (recurring lease revenue, ~42% EBITDA margin, $2.3B revenue), not a one-off custom-fabrication job shop. It trades **11.7x (5-yr low, Dec-2025) to 14.5x LTM EV/EBITDA, ~3.1x revenue.** I use it ONLY as the directional ceiling and to justify why ARTW's **project-based** modular deserves a *materially lower* multiple than a leasing compounder. Applying WSC's multiple to ARTW would be the classic comp error.
*Source: [WillScot Q4 CY2025 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001647088/000164708825000027/ex991-pressreleasexq12025.htm); [multiples.vc](https://multiples.vc/public-comps/willscot-valuation-multiples).*

### 3. Construction sector median — SECONDARY, the floor/reference band
Global construction EV/EBITDA median **~10.3x (2024)**; permanent-modular builders carry **~7% weighted EBITDA margin** (McKinsey). This frames the band: project-based building trades well below WSC's leasing multiple, but ARTW's modular segment runs ~17% op margin — *above* the construction-sector EBITDA-margin norm — which argues for the **upper half** of a project-based-builder range, not the bottom.
*Source: [Statista construction EV/EBITDA 2024](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1030142/); [McKinsey modular insight](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/engineering-construction-and-building-materials/our-insights/putting-the-pieces-together-unlocking-success-in-modular-construction).*

### Excluded: GVA (Granite) and the broad heavy-civil contractors
Documented and rejected — heavy-civil/infrastructure, no lab/biocontainment exposure, different cyclicality. Used only to confirm the broad-contractor band (~0.9x rev, ~9x EBITDA).

## Multiple chosen for the standalone modular segment
Given (i) ARTW modular's ~17% operating / ~20% EBITDA margin (well above the construction norm), (ii) a niche-leader position in biocontainment with high stated barriers to entry and a +103% YoY backlog, but (iii) project-based (lumpy, non-recurring) revenue, sub-scale ($10M), zero recurring lease base, and customer/funding concentration — I anchor the standalone modular EV/EBITDA at **bear 5x / base 7x / bull 10x**, with FONR's ~7–9x as the base-case sanity check and WSC's 11.7–14.5x as an explicit (unreachable, model-mismatched) ceiling. EV/revenue cross-check: bear ~0.7x / base ~1.1x / bull ~1.6x of ~$10–11M modular revenue.
