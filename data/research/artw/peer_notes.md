# ARTW Peer Selection Rationale

**Date:** 2026-05-30 · Updated 2026-05-31 (v3: FONR removed as a reference). For: Art's-Way Manufacturing (ARTW) Deep Flow carve-out thesis.

## The comp problem

ARTW has **no clean public pure-play comp.** It is two unlike businesses stapled together:
1. A cyclical, loss-making **farm-equipment** job shop (Ag Products), and
2. A profitable (~17% op margin), project-based **custom modular-building fabricator** specializing in research labs / biocontainment / animal facilities (Art's-Way Scientific).

The carve-out thesis values (2) standalone. The relevant universe is *specialty/custom modular building fabricators* and *project-based specialty contractors* — which has no tight US public pure-play. So we triangulate from industry anchors and document why each is partial. **We deliberately restrict the comp set to industry-/end-market-relevant references** (see the FONR exclusion below).

## The anchors (industry-relevant only)

### 1. Construction / specialty-builder sector median — PRIMARY reference band
Global construction EV/EBITDA median **~10.3x (2024)**; permanent-modular builders carry **~7% weighted EBITDA margin** (McKinsey). This frames the band: project-based building trades well below a leasing compounder, but ARTW's modular segment runs **~17% op / ~20% EBITDA margin — above the construction-sector norm** — which argues for a multiple discounted from the ~10x sector median for lumpiness/scale, **not** the bottom of the range.
*Source: [Statista construction EV/EBITDA 2024](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1030142/); [McKinsey modular insight](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/engineering-construction-and-building-materials/our-insights/putting-the-pieces-together-unlocking-success-in-modular-construction).*

### 2. WSC (WillScot) — the "modular" ceiling (with a big asterisk)
The only sizeable public "modular" name, but a **rental/leasing fleet** business (recurring lease revenue, ~42% EBITDA margin, $2.3B revenue), not a one-off custom-fabrication job shop. Trades **11.7x (5-yr low, Dec-2025) to 14.5x LTM EV/EBITDA, ~3.1x revenue.** Used ONLY as the directional ceiling and to justify why ARTW's **project-based** modular deserves a *materially lower* multiple than a leasing compounder.
*Source: [WillScot Q4 CY2025 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001647088/000164708825000027/ex991-pressreleasexq12025.htm); [multiples.vc](https://multiples.vc/public-comps/willscot-valuation-multiples).*

### 3. Biocontainment-modular specialists — qualitative premium support (private, no public mark)
The closest *end-market* peers — **CERTEK, Germfree, Modular Genius** — are private, so there is no public multiple. Qualitatively, the specialized biocontainment/BSL niche (engineering, code expertise, ~6-month delivery vs. 2–5 years conventional) supports ARTW's margin and a **premium to generic project builders**, while the absence of recurring lease revenue keeps it **below** WSC.

### Excluded references (documented and rejected)
- **GVA (Granite Construction)** — heavy-civil/infrastructure, no lab/biocontainment exposure, different cyclicality. Used only to confirm the broad-contractor band (~0.9x rev, ~9x EBITDA).
- **FONR (Fonar Corp) — REMOVED as a reference (v3, 2026-05-31).** Fonar is a **diagnostic-imaging (MRI) company with zero end-market or business-model overlap** with a specialty modular builder. It appeared in earlier (v1/v2) notes as a "precedent transaction" anchor only because it was a separately-screened micro-cap take-private — i.e., a name we happened to analyze, **not an industry comp.** Anchoring ARTW's multiple or acquisition premium on it is not rigorous, so it is excluded. The **multiple** now rests on the construction/specialty-builder band (#1) capped by WSC (#2); the **acquisition premium** rests on the controlled-company freeze-out literature (see `acquisition_premium_comps.md`), not on FONR's deal-specific 31.5%.

## Multiple chosen for the standalone modular segment
Given (i) ARTW modular's ~17% operating / ~20% EBITDA margin (above the construction norm), (ii) a niche-leader position in biocontainment with high stated barriers to entry and a +103% YoY backlog, but (iii) project-based (lumpy, non-recurring) revenue, sub-scale ($10M), zero recurring lease base, and customer/funding concentration — anchor the standalone modular EV/EBITDA at **bear 5x / base 7x / bull 10x**: a discount from the ~10.3x construction-sector median for lumpiness/scale, an explicit (model-mismatched) ceiling at WSC's 11.7–14.5x, and a qualitative premium to generic builders for the biocontainment niche. EV/revenue cross-check: bear ~0.7x / base ~1.1x / bull ~1.6x of ~$10–11M modular revenue. The standalone **DCF independently corroborates** the base (~$12–14M modular EV), so the valuation does not depend on any single transaction anchor.
