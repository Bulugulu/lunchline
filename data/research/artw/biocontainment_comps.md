# Biocontainment / Modular-Cleanroom / Specialty-Lab-Construction Valuation Comps

**Purpose:** Find the best available valuation proxy (EV/EBITDA, EV/sales, transaction multiples) to anchor the multiple for **Art's-Way Scientific** — the modular-buildings segment of Art's-Way Manufacturing (ARTW). Segment designs/builds custom research and biocontainment laboratories (BSL-3 labs, vivariums) and animal-biosecurity buildings. ~$10M revenue, ~17% operating / ~20% EBITDA margin, **project-based (not leasing)**.

**Current anchor (pre-research):** construction-sector median ~10.3× EV/EBITDA discounted to a **7× base**; WillScot (modular *leasing*, 11.7–14.5×) as explicit unreachable ceiling; closest pure-plays (CERTEK, Germfree, Modular Genius) private with no public multiple.

**Research date:** 2026-05-31. All figures marked **[CITED]** (sourced figure) or **[EST]** (estimate/derived). Proxy quality flagged throughout.

## 3. Market size & growth (niche quality)

- **Modular cleanroom market:** ~$4.3B (2024) → ~$6.4B (2030), **~6.9% CAGR**; alt. estimates $7.6B→$10.4B at 5.5% and ~$8.9B by 2030 at 7.0%. US ~$2.0B (2024). Medical/pharma ≈ 28% of global. [CITED] [strategicmarketresearch.com](https://www.strategicmarketresearch.com/market-report/modular-cleanroom-market)
- **Read-through:** mid-single-digit growth — **better than generic construction (~GDP/low-single-digit) but not a hypergrowth secular story.** Biocontainment/BSL-3/vivarium is a thinner, defense- and biosafety-driven sub-niche with high regulation, few qualified builders (Germfree, CERTEK, ARTW, AES), and lumpy government/university demand. The *scarcity of qualified builders* — not market growth — is what supports above-construction-average margins (ARTW ~20% EBITDA vs Exyte ~7%).

## 5. Recommendation

**Does the biocontainment/cleanroom niche command a premium to generic ~10× construction?** **Partially — on margin and scarcity, yes; on multiple, only modestly, and the case is more nuanced than "niche = higher multiple."**

The evidence cuts both ways:
- **Supports a premium:** ARTW Scientific's ~20% EBITDA margin vs Exyte's ~7% and generic construction's mid-single-digits reflects a genuinely higher-value, scarce-builder niche (BSL-3/vivarium/biosecurity; only ~4 credible US peers, all private). Life-science/containment *products* and *services* clear 12–25×. Mid-single-digit, regulation-driven, defense-backed demand beats generic construction.
- **Caps the premium:** ARTW is **project-based, not leasing/recurring** — so it cannot earn WillScot's (~11.7×) or Limbach's *current* (~15×) recurring-driven multiples. The honest private-market clearing range for small, project-based, illiquid specialty-construction businesses is **~4–7× EBITDA** (BMI HVAC/plumbing 4.6× median, up to ~7.9×; HEPACO ~7× post-synergy). Comfort Systems' ~25× is a scale/AI-hype artifact, irrelevant to a $10M segment.

**Defensible EV/EBITDA range for ARTW's modular-lab segment: ~6×–9×, base ~7.5–8×.**

- **Floor (~6×):** small-cap, project-based, illiquid private specialty-construction (BMI HVAC/plumbing median ~4.6× plus a niche/margin premium).
- **Base (~7.5–8×):** anchored on **Limbach's pre-recurring ~9.5× project-era median, haircut for ARTW's micro-scale, customer concentration, lumpy government demand, and private-company illiquidity**, then *credited back* for the genuine biosafety-niche margin/scarcity premium. This lands modestly above the generic small-cap construction multiple but well below the diversified mid-caps.
- **Ceiling (~9×):** justified only if one weights the niche scarcity and ~20% margins heavily and leans on the life-science/containment demand premium; still below WillScot's leasing ceiling.

**Is the current 7× base too low? Marginally — yes.** The data supports nudging the base to **~7.5–8×** (not the 10.3× generic-construction median, and certainly not WillScot/Limbach-current levels). The upgrade rationale is **margin quality and builder scarcity in biosafety**, not market growth (only ~7% CAGR) and not recurring revenue (ARTW has none). A 7× base is defensible-conservative; **~8×** is the better-supported point estimate, with 6×–9× as the sensitivity band. Anything ≥10× would require either recurring/leasing economics or scale ARTW does not have.

**Honesty flags:** No disclosed pure-play transaction multiple exists (Germfree/AES deal values undisclosed) — the strongest direct comps are unquantified. The 8× recommendation is **triangulated** from (a) Limbach's project-era median, (b) BMI private specialty-construction multiples, and (c) the documented margin/scarcity premium of the biosafety niche — not from a single clean comp.
