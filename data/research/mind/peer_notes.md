# MIND Technology — Peer Selection Rationale

**Date:** 2026-05-29 · **Subject EV:** ~$45.6M (mkt cap $62.8M − net cash $17.96M) · **FY26 (yr-end 2026-01-31) rev $40.9M, adj EBITDA $5.3M**

## The comp problem
MIND sits at the intersection of three distinct valuation regimes, and which peer set you anchor to *is* the thesis:
1. **Declining marine/land seismic-equipment** (GEOS, Sercel/Viridien) — low multiples, cyclical, oil-&-gas-survey-levered. This is what the bear says MIND is.
2. **Profitable, growing, defense-levered underwater acoustics/sonar** (Coda Octopus, Kraken Robotics) — high multiples, secular tailwind. This is what the bull says MIND could re-rate into.
3. MIND's *actual* present state: a profitable-but-shrinking, ~$41M-revenue, ~60%-aftermarket marine-instrument maker with an *aspirational* (not-yet-monetized) defense/maritime-security angle. Management itself said maritime-security applications have generated **no revenue yet** (FY26 10-K) and have "less direct impact on us today" (Q4 call).

## Peers used
- **Coda Octopus (CODA) — PRIMARY, the single best comp.** Micro-cap underwater sonar + defense engineering + acoustic sensors/materials. ~$143M mkt cap, ~$28M revenue growing 33%, profitable, net cash. It trades at ~4-5x revenue / ~22-26x EBITDA *because it is growing and has genuine recurring defense revenue.* MIND is the value, un-proven, shrinking version of the same archetype. The gap between CODA's multiple and MIND's ~1.1x EV/rev is the re-rate prize **if** MIND converts its defense/maritime-security pipeline — and the warning that the market only pays up *after* the defense revenue is real, not before.
- **Kraken Robotics (KRKNF) — PRIMARY (directional), the aspirational ceiling.** ~$2B EV, 12.8x EV/rev, 56.8x EV/EBITDA, 24% EBITDA margin, growing. Defense-funded marine robotics/sonar. Used only to size the *bull re-rate optionality*; explicitly **not** used to set MIND's target multiple — its growth and defense mix are in a different universe.
- **Geospace Technologies (GEOS) — PRIMARY (end-market reality check).** Marine/land seismic acquisition + ocean-bottom nodes. ~$107M mkt cap, ~$101M revenue down 13%, **net loss -$29M.** The declining-oilfield-seismic future the bear underwrites — and notably GEOS is now *loss-making* while MIND stayed profitable at a fraction of the size. Anchors the downside multiple (~0.6-0.8x EV/rev for a melting seismic-equipment book).
- **Viridien / Sercel (VIRI) — SECONDARY.** Scale seismic-equipment + data. ~4-5x EV/EBITDA (levered). Confirms that mid-single-digit EBITDA multiples are the *structural norm* for cyclical seismic-equipment, capping MIND's base multiple far below the CODA/Kraken defense comps.

## Excluded
- **Oceaneering (OII)** — subsea *services*/ROV, $2B+, energy-services beta; different model and scale.

## How this maps to the MIND multiple
- **Bear:** GEOS-like, ~0.7-1.0x EV/rev / ~5-6x cyclical-trough EBITDA — a shrinking seismic-equipment book.
- **Base:** a profitable, niche, ~60%-aftermarket marine-instrument maker deserves *more* than melting GEOS but *far less* than growing CODA — ~6-8x adj EBITDA / ~1.0-1.3x EV/rev.
- **Bull:** partial CODA re-rate (~2-3x EV/rev / ~10-14x EBITDA) **only if** the defense/maritime-security and scientific-vessel pipeline converts into recurring revenue. Kraken's 12.8x is the theoretical ceiling, not a base.

**Caveats:** CODA EV/EBITDA and EV/rev are *estimated* (mkt cap less net cash ÷ estimated EBITDA); Viridien multiples are approximate from 2025 results commentary. All marked in the CSV. MIND's own "EV/EBITDA" depends on whether you use mgmt adj EBITDA ($5.3M → ~8.6x) or GAAP-style EBITDA (~$3.7M → ~12.3x).
