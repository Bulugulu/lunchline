Strongest Counter & Rebuttal

Bear: "Your variant IS the IR deck"

Management explicitly pitches collectibles / Alliance Authentic / Endstate NFC on every call. Redchip runs investor webinars on this exact thesis. A variant that mirrors management framing is distribution of consensus, not variant perception.

The thesis does not require the operational story. Pillars 1–3 are structural. Management cannot pitch "you're using the wrong GICS code" or "quants will clear us in 14 months." Collectibles is upside optionality, not the base case.

Other risks (from adversarial)

  • Distributor moat is thin. 13.8% GM, 0.5% FCF margin — even on the right comp, ceiling is 0.4–0.6x EV/Rev, not 1x.
  • Customer concentration in 3 names. Walmart/Amazon/Best Buy = 40% of sales; planogram reviews quarterly.
  • Dilution math at upside. Bull case at $20 implies 70.97M shares (vs. 50.97M today); the "3x off $6.34" headline is actually 2x including dilution.
