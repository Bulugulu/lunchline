A 12-month search to acquire an executive search firm

01Targets

Setting an explicit target for each stage of the pipeline gives us a number to manage against, which sharpens both our results and our accountability through the search.

Time to close
12 months
Signed & closed acquisition vs. 18–30 mo traditional benchmark.
Reply rate target
~20%
Blended multi-channel response. High by design: a small, high-value universe is worked warm, repeated, and referral-supported.
Sourcing cadence
~75/mo
≈18 researched firms/week. Quality over volume on a finite universe. Derived below.

Total addressable market
  • ~2,500–3,500 employer executive-search firms in the US.
  • Filter to retained boutiques at $1–10M EBITDA: ~500–1,000 nationally, a few hundred in the Northeast.
  • Because the universe is small, reaching the target LOIs and a close depends on a high conversion rate, not volume: the ~20% reply target is what carries the funnel.
